# E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project performs a complete Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist. The objective is to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, seller performance, payment preferences, and geographic sales distribution to generate actionable business insights.

## Business Objective

The goal of this project is to help management understand:

* Which product categories generate the highest revenue
* Which regions contribute the most sales
* Which customers provide the highest business value
* Customer purchasing behavior and trends
* Seller performance
* Payment method preferences
* Product and category review performance
* Revenue trends over time

## Dataset

Dataset Source:
Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist

The dataset contains information about:

* Customers
* Orders
* Order Items
* Products
* Payments
* Reviews
* Sellers
* Geographic Information

## Key Findings

* A small number of product categories generate the majority of revenue.
* Certain regions contribute significantly more sales than others.
* High-value customers generate a disproportionate share of revenue.
* Revenue shows seasonal trends across time periods.
* Seller performance varies significantly across the marketplace.
* Payment preferences influence purchasing behavior.

## Business Recommendations

1. Increase investment in high-performing product categories.
2. Strengthen relationships with top-performing sellers.
3. Improve customer retention programs.
4. Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue regions.
5. Monitor low-rated categories for quality improvements.
